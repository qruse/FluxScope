## 3-Line TL;DR

- iPhone 18 Pro is about 33% faster than the 17 Pro in one published graphics benchmark, which is real but not a promise for every game
- Its 62.8% stress-test stability only compares the phone with its own best run, so it says nothing about speed against another phone
- At $1,199, I would want evidence for the game and the problem I actually have before replacing a 17 Pro

*Apple US 256GB starting price, before tax and trade-in credits, and an analysis of published tests rather than our own gaming review*

## The GPU got faster, my game still has to notice

- Tom's Guide measured 46.4 fps on the 17 Pro and 61.93 fps on the 18 Pro in 3DMark Solar Bay Unlimited ([Tom's Guide review](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/iphone-18-pro-and-pro-max-review))
- That is about 33% faster in a fixed graphics workload, not the frame rate of any named game
- Apple says its new 7-core GPU is up to 40% faster than the A19 Pro ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/))
- Apple also credits a redesigned vapor chamber with up to 40% better sustained performance
- Both are reasons to investigate, not reasons to skip the investigation
- A faster chip still cannot add a missing frame-rate option to a game's menu by looking expensive

| Published measurement | 17 Pro | 18 Pro | What it means |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Solar Bay Unlimited average | 46.4 fps | 61.93 fps | About 33% faster in this test |
| Wild Life Extreme stress stability | 61.1% | 62.8% | Retention up 1.7 percentage points |

![iPhone GPU frame rates and retention ratios kept separate across different tests](/images/posts/it-devices/iphone-18-pro-gaming-benchmarks.webp)

## Similar percentages do not mean similar speed

- UL defines stability as the worst loop divided by the best loop, times 100 ([UL explanation](https://support.benchmarks.ul.com/support/solutions/articles/44002134931-stress-test-result-screen))
- It compares a phone with its own best run, not with the phone next to it
- Picture one device falling from 100 to 60 and another from 150 to 90, with made-up numbers

![Hypothetical scores of 100 to 60 and 150 to 90 share 60 percent retention but differ in absolute performance](/images/posts/it-devices/stability-ratio-example.webp)

- Both keep 60%, yet 90 is still faster than 60
- So 61.1% versus 62.8% cannot prove that long-session speed barely improved
- Multiplying Solar Bay fps by Wild Life stability does not invent a sustained frame rate either
- The calculator will happily do it, because nobody told it these are two different tests
- The Pro Max climbs from 65.2% to 79.4%, but cooling headroom is not included in Family Sharing

## The upgrade test starts with your current phone

- Apple lists the 256GB 18 Pro from $1,199 in the US ([Apple Store](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-18-pro))
- Name the complaint the new phone should fix before you spend it

| Current complaint | Evidence to look for |
| :--- | :--- |
| The game will not offer the frame-rate mode you want | Support for that game, version and device |
| Frame rate drops later in a session | Long runs at the same settings, brightness and room temperature |
| Heat or battery drain is the real problem | Temperature, power or battery data next to the frame rate |

- For a happy 17 Pro owner, this benchmark alone is a weak case to replace the phone
- For an older-phone owner with a specific problem, it is a lead worth following
- Wanting a new phone is a perfectly good reason on its own
- Asking a benchmark to dress that wish up as a necessity is where the paperwork gets creative

## Q&A (Field Notes)

- **Q: Does 61.93 fps mean a demanding game will hold 60 fps?**
  - No, it is Solar Bay's average, and each game's settings, cap and sustained behavior need their own measurement
- **Q: Does 62.8% stability prove the phone runs cooler?**
  - No, retention is not a temperature reading, so it needs thermal and performance data alongside it
- **Q: Should I pick the Pro Max for gaming because of its better stability?**
  - It is worth comparing, but absolute game performance, size and battery still decide the purchase
