## The short answer

Humanoid robotics is shifting from handcrafted control pipelines toward Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models. By tokenizing sensory inputs and joint actuators into a unified transformer architecture, robots learn generalizable physical manipulation directly from human demonstrations and synthetic physics simulations.
