## 3-Line TL;DR

- The eighth-generation Avante keeps physical climate and hazard buttons beside its large display, with more rear-seat room; changing the temperature need not become a touchscreen treasure hunt ([Hyundai release](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/ko/news/hyundai-motor-company-all-new-avante))
- Gasoline entry pricing moves from the previous **₩20.62m Smart** to the new **₩23.98m Modern**, a **₩3.36m higher entry ticket** across different engines and standard equipment ([old price PDF](https://www.hyundai.com/contents/repn-car/catalog/avante-2026-price.pdf), [new release](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/ko/news/hyundai-motor-company-all-new-avante))
- Modern-to-Modern is **₩23.88m to ₩23.98m**, only ₩0.10m apart; the twist behind that ₩3.36m headline is the missing budget Smart trim

## Interior: the tablet grew and the buttons survived

[Hyundai describes](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/ko/news/hyundai-motor-company-all-new-avante) a symmetrical cockpit, double-D steering wheel, large center display, and physical controls for climate and hazard functions. Keeping frequent controls within reach is a more persuasive design choice than simply enlarging a screen

- **12.9- or 14.6-inch Pleos Connect** center displays and the app market are standard across trims; check the exact display and options on the selected trim ([Hyundai release](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/ko/news/hyundai-motor-company-all-new-avante))
- [Hyundai's Tech Day](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/ko/story/the-all-new-avante-tech-day) lists rear legroom at **964 → 993mm** and headroom at **940 → 958mm**, aided by slimmer seatbacks
- A pretty dash does not prove usable controls in motion; surviving climate buttons do not grant every menu a free pass. Inspect reach, screen response, and cluster options in a real vehicle

## Price: open both price sheets before shouting “₩3.36m hike”

| Comparison | Previous 2026 model | All-new model | Difference |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lowest gasoline entry | Smart 1.6 **₩20.62m** | Modern 2.0 **₩23.98m** | **+₩3.36m** |
| Modern nameplate | Modern 1.6 **₩23.88m** | Modern 2.0 **₩23.98m** | **+₩0.10m** |
| New upper gasoline trims | Not compared | Premium **₩27.71m**, Inspiration **₩31.52m** | Options extra |

- Old Smart and new Modern have **different engines, equipment, and trim positions**; the ₩3.36m entry gap is real, but treating them as identical cars makes the calculator look guilty
- [Hyundai lists](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/ko/news/hyundai-motor-company-all-new-avante) Pleos Connect, ten airbags, and Lane Following Assist 2 as standard on new Modern, while the cheaper entry choice has vanished
- Hybrid Modern starts at **₩30.42m before tax benefits** in Hyundai's announcement; the advertised starting price and your option-filled checkout are different genres

## Community Reactions

- **Praise for the outside, brakes on the cabin**: In overseas Elantra/Avante launch discussions, several commenters liked the exterior but disliked the floating center screen. Photos do not establish how it feels to operate, yet the layout plainly divides opinions
- **Physical buttons as a rare compromise**: Other commenters disliked the large display but welcomed the remaining basic controls. That supports looking closely at the buttons without pretending one discussion is a poll of every buyer
- **The cluster deserves a separate price check**: An overseas discussion also focused on driver-display options in the new Elantra. North American equipment does not establish Korean trim content; local buyers should check the actual Korean price sheet

## Q&A (Field Notes)

- **Q: Did every Avante become ₩3.36m more expensive?**
  - No; that is the gap between two different lowest-priced gasoline trims with different powertrains and equipment
- **Q: Can cabin photos replace a showroom visit?**
  - No; control reach, visibility, display response, and rear-seat space need a real vehicle check; this is an analysis of published material, not a test drive
- **Q: Is ₩30.42m the hybrid drive-away price?**
  - No; Hyundai labels it a pre-tax-benefit price, before selected options and registration costs
