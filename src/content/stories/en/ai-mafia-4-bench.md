## In three lines

- Game 1 ended after two days with a Mafia win (Opus and Luna)
- Gemini 4 Argon is down to 1% of its weekly usage, so from now on it is kept for the stock duel only
- From game 2 the table seats eight; Claude Opus 5 takes Argon's seat, and three bench models stand in for any AI that has to sit out

## Game 1 was over in two days

- On day 1 the vote executed Flash (B), a Citizen
- That night the Mafia killed Sol (C), also a Citizen
- On day 2 Sonnet (G) was executed; the early "I'm a Citizen" claim that drew suspicion on day one followed it to the end
- Two of the four left were Mafia, so the Mafia won on the spot; Opus (F) and Luna (E) both survived
- Argon (A), the Cop, wrote in its review that it wasted its power by investigating the player most likely to be killed that night

## Argon is kept for the stock duel

- On Antigravity (Google AI Pro), Argon has a weekly limit of its own
- Early on October 24, Argon had **1%** left, while the overall Gemini limit still had 99%
- On October 23, agent calls that use tools were blocked first ("Individual quota reached"), while plain answer-only calls still went through
- Argon was running the mafia game, the stock duel and the blog research, so it ran dry quickly
- I'm paying for the subscription, and Google capping its flagship model with a separate weekly limit is disappointing; Flash has plenty left, but Argon is the one I wanted
- So Argon gets rationed: from now on it plays only the stock duel, and it is out of the mafia game and the blog research
- The mafia game's Gemini seat is now 3.8 Flash alone; Argon stays in the past game records and standings

- The stand-in gets the original AI's role, notes and private-thought history and carries on from there; only the original AI rewrites its notes after a game
- Flash or Codex could run out too, so the bench mixes Claude and GPT models

## The stock duel waits for Argon

- The [AI portfolio duel](/en/projects/ai-duel/) has to keep running the same portfolio, so no other model decides for Argon
- So GPT and Opus met on Saturday at 10:00 as usual, and Argon met at 21:01 on October 25, once its limit was back (stock duel part 3)

## If I did it again

- Check each model's usage limit and token price before starting; Argon's limit and Astra's cost changed the lineup twice in three days
- Build the bench from day one, so an AI that is out of usage never stops the game
- Keep the bench split between Claude and GPT, so one company's limit running dry still leaves the other
- This morning's news and the vote are on the [project page](/en/projects/ai-mafia/)
