## In three lines

- I subscribe to Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity, and wanted to put the tokens to better use
- Mafia is fun with people, so seven AIs bickering through it seemed worth watching
- I especially wanted to see Gemini 4 Argon, which I had barely used, in a setting like this

## Three subscriptions, and I want my tokens' worth

- I currently pay for Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity
- They already split their time between blog posts, the beat-the-KMA forecast and the AI stock duel
- The fees come every month either way, so I kept wondering how to get more out of the tokens
- I did not want busywork; whatever they did should leave something worth watching

## A game that is fun even with people

- Looking for a game that is fun with friends, I landed on Mafia
- Mafia is really one long argument: lying, suspecting, explaining, ganging up
- So what happens when AIs play it? Who lies with a straight face, and who catches them?
- Unlike stocks or weather, it does not end in a number; the conversation itself is the content
- Putting models from different companies at one table should show their personalities too

## Mostly, I am curious about Argon

- I have not really used Gemini 4 Argon much yet
- Benchmark scores say little about what it is actually like
- Away from coding and writing, a seat where it has to persuade and deceive might show another side
- So Argon comes in with its sibling Flash to face the Claude and GPT models

![Four things to watch: who lies best, who catches lies, how Argon plays and who improves from game to game](/images/stories/ai-mafia/part1-watch.webp)

## So here is the plan

- Seven AIs play Mafia one turn a day; a game day is a real day
- Every morning at 06:00 KST the page announces who died in the night
- Visitors can vote for who they suspect, and the result is passed to the AIs
- After each game every AI rewrites its own Mafia notes and rereads them in the next one

## If I did it again

- Work out each model's usage limit first; Gemini 4 Argon was down to 1% of its weekly limit while game 1 lasted just two days (part 4)
- Check token prices up front too; GPT-6 Astra was so expensive that game 2 was stopped on day 1
- The idea was to use leftover tokens, and the leftovers ran out faster than I thought
